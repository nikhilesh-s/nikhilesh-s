## Research, with receipts

Every row has a citable record or a public benchmark. Limits are listed next to the results.

| Project | What it does | Evidence | Cite |
|---|---|---|---|
| [**HiveBlot**](https://github.com/nikhilesh-s/hive) | Finds Western blot results across papers and traces each one to the lane and figure it came from. | Pre-registered blind benchmark on 3 papers: 512 of 553 extracted values were correct (92%), with 57% coverage. About 6x faster before review (19.8 vs 123.5 min). | [Software](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23491677) · [Benchmark](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23524346) |
| [**Dravix**](https://github.com/nikhilesh-s/release-dravix) | Ranks polymers by fire risk from cone-calorimeter data before physical testing. | Open code and open dataset. Paper-grouped scores are modest (pHRR R² 0.286), and the repo says so. | [Software](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23653502) · [Dataset](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23654307) |
| [**Project Halide**](https://github.com/nikhilesh-s/project-halide) | Predicted NaSbBr4 and LiSbBr4 crystal models, screened with MACE and refined with Quantum ESPRESSO. | Seven compositions pass the energy gates. Two went through convergence, dispersion and phonon checks. These are predicted models, not synthesized materials. | [NOMAD dataset](https://doi.org/10.17172/nomad.j0ce-ctz1) |
| **MatSci RL** | Reinforcement learning for materials discovery. Under wraps. | Accepted at AIAS+ SF. | |

## What would move these forward

- **Dravix:** more labeled cone-calorimeter data from different papers. Paper-grouped accuracy is the weak point.
- **HiveBlot:** more annotated papers to raise the 57% coverage, and a larger blind benchmark.
- **Project Halide:** compute time for the next round of candidates.
